Direct review link: in GBP, Home, "Ask for reviews" gives you a short link. Paste it here and in your phone's text templates:

```
REVIEW_LINK: https://g.page/r/REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_CODE/review
```

Text or email script, send the day of the final walkthrough:

```
Hi [Name], thanks again for trusting us with your [kitchen / basement / addition]. If you have two minutes, a Google review helps other Northern Colorado homeowners find us: [REVIEW_LINK]. If anything is not right, call me first at (970) 430-5884 and I will fix it. Doug, Standard Building Company
```

Reply to every review within two days. For a five-star review, thank them and name the project type and town (that text helps local ranking). For anything under four stars, reply once, briefly, offer a call, and take it offline.

Target: one new review per completed project, five reviews in the first 60 days.

## 10. Google Local Services Ads (note only, nothing enabled)

Local Services Ads are the "Google Guaranteed" units that appear above the map pack for searches like "general contractor Loveland" and "kitchen remodeler Fort Collins." They are the fastest paid lever for a local contractor because you pay per lead rather than per click, the units outrank every organic result, and the Google Guaranteed badge converts well. Qualification requires a background check, proof of general liability insurance, and the contractor license each jurisdiction requires, which is the same documentation the website now says you provide on request. Budgets are set per week and can be paused any time. Nothing has been enabled; if you decide to try it, start at https://ads.google.com/local-services-ads after the GBP work above is done, because LSA pulls reviews and categories from the profile.
